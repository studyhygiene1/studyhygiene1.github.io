Cancellation & Refund Policy

We strive to provide an exceptional customer service experience to all our clients. In order to provide a consistently high quality of service, and ensure customer satisfaction across the site, the following cancellation and refund policies apply across all of Hanees Tutors' services.

Cancelling or Rescheduling a Lesson

All lessons can be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge at least 24 hours before the scheduled start time. Cancellations or rescheduling made with less than 24 hours notice results in the lesson being forfeited.

Ending a Lesson Early

If a student chooses to end a lesson early, for any reason, the remaining lesson time is forfeited.

No Show

If a student misses a lesson, for any reason, it is forfeited. A student is considered a "no-show" when 15 mins of the lesson time has passed and the student has not initiated any contact to notify tutor of a late arrival.

Weekly Preparation Packages

Weekly Preparation packages (ASVAB, ACT, SAT, and Math) are non-refundable once purchased, and expire 10 days from the date of purchase. Lessons not used within that window are forfeited.
