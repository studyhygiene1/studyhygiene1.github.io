Cancellation & Refund Policy

We strive to provide all our clients with exceptional customer service. In order to provide a consistently high level of service, keep costs affordable, and ensure customer satisfaction, the following cancellation and refund policies apply across all of Hanees Tutors' services. By booking a lesson with Hanees Tutors, you agree to the following policies.

Lesson Expiration

All lessons are non-refundable. Hourly and weekly lessons expire 10 days from the date of purchase and in the event they need to be rescheduled to a later date, they must be used before the 10-day expiration date.

Rescheduling a Lesson

All lessons are non-refundable. However, any scheduled lesson can be rescheduled 24 hours or more before the scheduled start time, free of charge. Once the lesson is less than 24 hours away from its scheduled start time, the lesson may be cancelled (without a refund) but cannot be rescheduled.

Cancelling a Lesson

Any lesson may be cancelled at any time without a refund.

Late Arrival or Ending a Lesson Early

If a student arrives late or chooses to end a lesson early, for any reason, the lost lesson time is forfeited.

No Show

If a student misses a lesson, for any reason, it is forfeited. A student is considered a "no-show" if the student is absent 15 mins after the lesson's start time without prior notification to us of a late arrival.

Weekly Preparation Packages

Weekly packages (ASVAB, ACT, SAT, and Math) are non-refundable once purchased, and expire 10 days from the date of purchase. Lessons not used within that time window are forfeited.
