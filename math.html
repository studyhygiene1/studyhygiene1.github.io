Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Are these math lessons one-on-one?

Yes. All tutoring is private, one-on-one online instruction tailored to your specific course and goals.

When and where are lessons held?

Lessons are conducted online using Google Meet and scheduled according to your needs and convenience. We provide services 12 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Which option should I choose?

If you're unsure, start with a free 15-minute consultation, or book your $30 First Lesson to try a full session and get personalized feedback on your current level. For ongoing support, the Weekly Preparation package is our most popular option.

Does the weekly package expire?

Yes. The Weekly Preparation package is non-refundable once purchased and expires 10 days from purchase. See our full Cancellation & Refund Policy for details.

What courses do you cover?

Arithmetic (K–8), Algebra I, Geometry, Algebra II, Trigonometry, Pre-Calculus, and Calculus (Calculus I & II, AP Calculus AB/BC, and Business Calculus).

Do I need to know exactly what I'm struggling with before starting?

No. We can discuss your course, goals, and problem areas during a free consultation or your first lesson.

How do I get started?

Click any of the Get Started or package buttons on this page. A short intake form will open.

How do I pay?

Payment is handled directly. We'll contact you to discuss available payment options.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

Your $30 First Lesson is non-refundable, but there's no obligation to continue afterward. The Weekly Preparation package is also non-refundable once purchased and expires 10 days from purchase. Lessons can be cancelled or rescheduled with at least 24 hours notice — less notice results in the lesson being forfeited. See our full Cancellation & Refund Policy for details.
