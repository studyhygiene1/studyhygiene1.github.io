Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Are these ASVAB programs one-on-one?

Yes. All tutoring is private, one-on-one online instruction tailored to the student's needs and goals.

When and where are lessons held?

Lessons are conducted online using Google Meet and scheduled according to the student's needs and convenience. We provide services 12 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Which preparation option should I choose?

If you're unsure, start with a free 15-minute consultation, or book your Trial Lesson and Diagnostic to experience a full session and get personalized feedback on where to focus.

How much preparation time do you recommend?

Each student's needs are different. A free consult or a Trial Lesson and Diagnostic can help you choose the right package. Generally, we recommend allowing approximately 4–8 weeks for preparation. If you wish to proceed with a package without a consult or full evaluation, we recommend the Weekly Preparation option. This gives you the best value, and in our experience, produces the highest scores.

What if my ASVAB test date is strict and cannot be moved?

Accelerated options are available, including a 7-Day ASVAB Crash Course for $745 and a 2-Week ASVAB Intensive for $1,195.

How does weekly preparation work?

Weekly ASVAB Preparation includes 4 private 1-hour lessons per week for $380, saving compared with the individual lesson rate.

Do I need to know my target score before starting?

No. We can discuss your goals and testing timeline during a free consultation or diagnostic session.

How do I get started?

Click any of the Get Started or package buttons on this page. A short intake form will open.

How do I pay?

Payment is handled directly. We'll contact you to discuss available payment options.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

Your Trial Lesson and Diagnostic is non-refundable, but there's no obligation to continue afterward. Packages are also non-refundable once purchased. Lessons can be cancelled or rescheduled with at least 24 hours notice — less notice results in the lesson being forfeited. See our full Cancellation & Refund Policy for details.
