Getting Started

How do online tutoring sessions work?

Sessions are live and one-on-one, conducted online using Google Meet, and scheduled according to your needs and availability. We provide services 12 hours a day, 7 days a week.

How do I get started?

Click any "Get Started" or package button on this site to book directly online, or reach out directly by email or text. We'll confirm your session details right after you book.

Do you offer a trial or introductory session?

Yes. New students can book a free 15-minute consultation to ask questions and discuss goals, or book a full First Lesson for $30 — a complete 1-hour session that includes a free diagnostic assessment, with no obligation to continue afterward.

Which preparation option should I choose?

If you're unsure, start with a free 15-minute consultation, or book your $30 First Lesson to experience a full session and get personalized feedback on where to focus.

How much time should I allow for preparation?

Every student's needs are different, and this varies by subject and goals — a free consult or your First Lesson can help you figure out the right timeline. If you'd rather skip that step, the Weekly Preparation package is generally our recommended default: it gives the best value and, in our experience, produces the strongest results.

Do I need to know my current level or target score before starting?

No. We can talk through your goals, current level, and timeline during a free consultation or your First Lesson.

Can I switch subjects partway through, or combine subjects?

Yes. Many students work with Hanees across more than one subject area (for example, math support alongside SAT prep). Let us know what you need and we'll tailor a plan around it.

Scheduling, Payment & Policies

How is scheduling handled?

Session times are arranged directly based on your availability, with services offered 12 hours a day, 7 days a week. Both flexible hourly lessons and weekly preparation packages are available.

Payment is handled directly — we'll contact you to confirm available payment options once you enroll.

The $30 First Lesson and the Weekly Preparation packages are non-refundable once purchased. Individual lessons can be cancelled or rescheduled with at least 24 hours' notice — less notice results in the lesson being forfeited. Full details are on our Cancellation & Refund Policy page.

Does the Weekly Preparation package expire?

Yes — the Weekly Preparation package is valid for 10 days from the purchase date. See the Cancellation & Refund Policy page for full terms.
